About

I like making complicated systems feel legible.

My work sits between product thinking, operations, and interface design. I am most energized by problems where the underlying system is messy, high-stakes, or hard to explain, especially in finance. I like finding the friction, mapping the decision flow, and building something people can actually trust and use.

What I build

Prototypes, workflows, and decision-support interfaces that turn dense systems into cleaner experiences.

How I think

I start with constraints, user confusion, and process breakdowns before deciding what the product should do.

Why finance

Featured Project

Chase Bank App Prototype

A front-end-only banking prototype built to test whether simpler navigation and cleaner flows can reduce friction in everyday money tasks.

Problem

Mobile banking interfaces often bury important actions behind crowded navigation and habit-based patterns.

What I built

Two UX conditions, current versus simplified, using Vite, vanilla JavaScript, and CSS to compare navigation and interaction choices.

Why it matters

The project helped me explore how product clarity can change confidence, speed, and decision-making in financial tools.
