02 / note

Working note / short form

The possession isn’t the game.

A JEPA is like watching basketball from the sideline: learning spacing, movement, and the structure of play by predicting hidden representations, without reconstructing every pixel or being rewarded for choosing the next move. A base language model, by contrast, is typically trained to predict the next token; an instruction-following model may later be tuned on demonstrations and human-preference rewards. Neither objective is the game itself. The best-looking play for one possession can still be wrong for the final score. I keep returning to that gap: what a system optimizes now versus what we actually care about.
